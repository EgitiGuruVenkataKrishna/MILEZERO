/ 01 — The method

Less “learn everything.”
More do the right next thing.

Every answer should change the plan. If a question doesn’t affect the route, it doesn’t belong in the interview.

01 / READ THE START↗

Where you actually are.

Student or employee. Prior proof. Paid internship experience. Weekly time, not fantasy time.

02 / FIND THE LEVER↗

Choose the first win.

Personalised follow-ups find the constraint that changes sequencing, scope, and what to build first.

03 / BUILD THE PROOF↗

Leave with a route.

A milestone-led roadmap with tasks, free learning sources, proof standards, and progress you can track offline.

A roadmap should be something you use, not something you save and forget.

Your plan downloads as a standalone interactive HTML file. Check off tasks, open the resource links, and keep the file wherever you plan your week. No PDF. No account dashboard required to read it.

/ 02 — The standard

No fake certainty.
No course-collecting.

The system can organise a smarter route. It cannot promise an internship, a job offer, or a timeline the market won’t respect.

FILTER 01

Free before paid.

Prioritise official docs, public guides and genuinely free courses. Recommend only what the next task needs.

FILTER 02

Proof over badges.

Build projects, case studies, tests, demos or work samples that show what you can actually do.

FILTER 03

One decisive route.

The roadmap chooses a direction based on your answers. It does not bury you under seven options and call it personalisation.
